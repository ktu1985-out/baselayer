How points work

The start day scores 1 point and counts as active in every category, so logging on the start day doesn't change its score. Every later day looks at the 10 days before it.

No active day in those 10: an inactive day scores −0.5 and an active day scores 1, which restarts the habit. Otherwise an active day scores 1 point with 1–3 active days behind it, 1.25 with 4, 1.5 with 5, 2 with 6, and 3 with 7 or more. A day without activity scores 0.

The number at the end of each layer is what today is worth if you do it. The number under its name is the average points per day over the last 10 days.
